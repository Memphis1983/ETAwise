Our intended product principles
01 /

Evidence before inference.

Suggested commitments should point back to source messages. Missing information stays missing until someone confirms it.

02 /

An update isn't a fix.

A promised check-in is not a resolution ETA. Netdin is not being built to invent repair dates or engineer availability.

03 /

Assistance, not autopilot.

Your team reviews AI suggestions. Confirmed dates drive rules-based tracking, not unverified predictions.

05 A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Clarity starts
right here.

Is Netdin available to use today?+

Not yet. Netdin is in development. This website introduces the product direction; the interactive preview uses fictional data and does not run AI.

Does Netdin predict a resolution ETA?+

No. A promised update is not a promised fix. The planned product tracks confirmed actions and update commitments, not speculative resolution dates.

Will it replace our existing helpdesk?+

No. Netdin is intended to complement existing support workflows. The initial scope starts with pasted notes or conversation exports. Native integrations are a future direction, not a current feature.

Who is it being built for?+

Support operations teams, service-delivery managers, and businesses coordinating cases across frontline teams, specialists, and service partners. The workflow is not limited to one industry.

What will it cost?+

Pricing has not been announced. We are shaping the initial product scope before setting subscription plans. There are no payments or subscriptions on this website.
